Home / Our Core Divisions

Three technical divisions, structured for clarity.

Everything we procure and supply falls into one of three pillars, built on over 11 years of oil and gas material sourcing experience, so your enquiry reaches the right specification the first time.

HSE Systems & PPE MRO Supplies Access & Logistics
Division 01

HSE Systems & Personnel Protection Equipment

Specialized supply of high-specification personnel safety wear: head-to-toe protection assets compliant with international safety standards, including EN and ANSI classifications.

Core Deliverables

  • Heavy-duty site coveralls and hi-vis wear
  • Steel-toe safety boots
  • Impact-resistant gloves
  • Ballistic and safety eyewear
  • Fall-arrest harnesses

Our Specialty Integrated in-house branding

We provide precision industrial branding, custom color coding and embroidery for corporate safety gear before it leaves our facility, so subcontractor workforces mobilize to site fully compliant and identifiable from day one.

Division 02

Maintenance, Repair & Operations Supplies

Direct procurement pipelines for high-utilization site consumables and industrial tooling, built for facility turnaround and routine maintenance schedules.

Core Deliverables

  • Precision power tools, including authorized Bosch and INGCO configurations
  • Cutting and grinding discs
  • Industrial fasteners
  • Welding electrodes and consumables
  • Facility cleaning and janitorial chemistry

Non-sparking hand tools, including brass hammers, are available for gas-zone applications on request.

Division 03

Access & Logistics Infrastructure

Material support for structural access and site positioning, plus the haulage and delivery coordination that gets it there on schedule.

Core Deliverables

  • British Standard compliant scaffolding tubes
  • Heavy-duty right-angle and swivel clamps
  • Couplers and scaffold boards
  • Access towers and rope access equipment
  • Site-to-site haulage and delivery coordination

Not sure which division your request falls under?

Send us your material list. We will route it to the right specification and quote it directly.
