Who We Are

Greg Energy Limited is a procurement and supply company. We find, source and deliver the equipment industrial, oil & gas and construction teams depend on — reliably, on the terms that work for your project.

Our sourcing network isn't limited to a fixed catalogue — if it's equipment your site needs, tell us and we'll find it. We work on a purchase order, whether paid up front or on agreed terms after delivery, cash or cashless, depending on the scope. Alongside supply, we offer logistics to move what we source, oil & gas field support, and general contracting for adjacent industrial work.

What we source and supply.

Personal protective equipment

Machinery & power tools

Scaffolding & access equipment

Compressors & site equipment
