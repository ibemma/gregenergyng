Operational discipline: the purchase order framework.

How an order moves from request to delivery on site, backed by over 11 years of oil and gas material sourcing experience.

The PO Advantage

We understand the financial and administrative dynamics of heavy industries. Every order runs on a purchase order, with terms confirmed before we source.

Greg Energy Limited works to a disciplined purchase order framework. Payment terms are agreed for each order and depend on the scope and value of the procurement, so both sides know what to expect before any sourcing begins. Every order, whatever the division it falls under, moves through the same four stages below.

The Workflow

Four stages, from enquiry to site delivery.

 

1

RFQ & technical alignment

You submit a request for quote with your material specification. We respond rapidly with precise OEM part matching, so there is no ambiguity about what will arrive.

2

PO issuance

Your internal corporate purchase order is absorbed directly into our workflow. Terms are confirmed and formalized before sourcing begins.

3

Sourcing & QA/QC

Materials are pulled from verified local supply channels and checked for quality before they leave our facility.

4

Just-in-time delivery

Consolidated logistics deliver directly to your site footprint, whether that is Trans Amadi in Port Harcourt or a facility further afield.
