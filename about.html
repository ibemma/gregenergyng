Our Story

While formally incorporated in 2026 to scale our corporate capacity, Greg Energy Limited is built on an 11-year foundational track record of trust, logistics excellence and industrial supply in the Niger Delta.

For over a decade, our founding team has sourced material for operators across Nigeria's oil and gas sector, from wellheads and industrial pipes to machinery and site consumables. Incorporation gave that experience a corporate structure. It did not start the work; it scaled it.

We bridge the gap between global original equipment manufacturers and local operational fronts, ensuring critical equipment reaches the field without bureaucratic delay. Our sourcing network is not limited to a fixed catalogue: if it is equipment your site needs, tell us and we will find it.
